About Us

Darjeeling Sunrise Bike & Scooty Rental

Darjeeling Sunrise Bike & Scooty Rental is your trusted two-wheeler rental shop in the heart of Darjeeling. Located in the vibrant Chauk Bazaar area, we offer a well-maintained fleet of bikes and scooties so you can explore the hills, tea gardens, and viewpoints of Darjeeling at your own pace. With a stellar rating of 4.7 out of 5, our commitment to vehicle quality, fair pricing, and friendly service has earned the trust of hundreds of happy riders.

Whether you need a scooty for a quick day trip around town or a geared bike for a longer ride into the hills, our team makes the pickup and drop process quick and hassle-free. We are here every day from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM to get you on the road fast.
